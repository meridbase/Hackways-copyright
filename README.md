## About Hackways

Hackways is designed to provide a dedicated digital infrastructure for modern events and communities.

The platform enables people to discover events and gives organizers a professional environment to create, publish, brand, and manage them.

Instead of relying entirely on disconnected registration forms, spreadsheets, payment links, and communication channels, Hackways aims to bring the event experience into one platform.

```text
                    HACKWAYS
                       │
```

## Platform Philosophy

Hackways is built around several core principles.

### Event First

An event should be more than a registration form.

### Discovery First

People should be able to discover events based on what they are interested in.

### Organizer Ownership

Organizers should have a professional digital presence for their events.

### Community Driven

Events are an important mechanism for building communities, relationships, and experiences.

### Simple Experiences

Attendees should be able to discover an event and understand how to participate without unnecessary friction.

### Professional Presentation

Events should look and feel like real products and experiences rather than generic forms.

## The Problem

Traditional event workflows often look like:

```text
Social Media
     ↓
Registration Form
     ↓
Spreadsheet
     ↓
Payment Link
     ↓
WhatsApp / Email
     ↓
Manual Management
```

Hackways is designed around a more centralized experience:

```text
                 HACKWAYS
                    │
       ┌────────────┼────────────┐
       ↓            ↓            ↓
   Discover       Create       Manage
       │            │            │
       ↓            ↓            ↓
     Explore     Event Page   Registrations
       │            │            │
       └────────────┼────────────┘
                    ↓
                   RSVP
                    ↓
                  Attend
```
